How a request is handled

1 · Arrives
Client email or an internal chat message.
2 · Understood
Subject, client, urgency and required action identified.
3 · Ticket raised
Structured record with a target completion date.
4 · Routed
To physician, nursing or client care.
✋ Clinical check
Anything clinical, or anything uncertain, goes to a person instead.
5 · Tracked
Deadline watched daily; leadership alerted before it is missed.

Triage inbox

What the assistant concluded, and why

Every inbound message is read, classified and turned into a ticket. The reasoning is kept on the ticket so a person reviewing the queue can see why it landed where it did. A safety check runs after the assessment and can only ever move a message towards a human, never away from one.

Clinical safety boundary

The assistant coordinates work. It does not make clinical decisions, and it does not reply to clients. Every clinical or discretionary judgement is routed to a qualified person.

The assistant mayThe assistant may not
Identify a request as clinical and route itAssess whether treatment is appropriate
List which markers a panel is missingInterpret what a result means
Report that a follow-up is overdueAdvise on stopping or changing treatment
Escalate an unattended ticketReply to a client on the clinic's behalf
