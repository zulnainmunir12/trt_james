Hermes is an open-source, self-hostable agent framework (Nous Research, MIT licence). It provides the foundational architecture natively, allowing effort to focus on custom integrations and business logic rather than platform engineering. Each capability below is marked according to what has been demonstrated on this deployment.

Stage 1 and 2 of the operational workflow. Every inbound communication is read, interpreted and converted into a structured record. The reasoning is retained on the ticket so a reviewer can see why it was routed as it was — part of the audit trail.

Stage 3 — taxonomic routing. Ticket instances held in the central task repository, by designated functional role. Live.

Stages 4 and 5 — an automated routine audits open tickets against operational deadlines and dispatches alerts to team leadership before a target completion date is missed. Live.
